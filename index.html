📊 Project Overview

This project visualizes the IMDB Top 250 Movies Dataset. The dataset includes attributes such as movie title, year, rating, genre, runtime, directors, and writers.

⚠️ Note: Although the dataset includes budget and box_office fields, they are not consistently converted to USD. To ensure accuracy, these fields were excluded from analysis.

🔗 Data Sources

  • IMDB Top 250 Movies Dataset
  • Original IMDB List

📈 VizHub Visualizations

Scatter Plot: Rating vs. Runtime Scatter Plot: Year vs. Rating (with trend lines)

🧠 Week 10 Updates

This week, I continued developing the IMDB Top 250 Movies visualizations, focusing on adding more interactive and analytical features based on the theme of interaction.

  • Rating vs. Runtime Scatter Plot: Hover over items in the legend to highlight genres. Tooltips show movie title, director, and year.
  • Year vs. Rating Scatter Plot: Added genre-based trend lines per decade. Each line can be clicked to highlight and reveal average ratings.
