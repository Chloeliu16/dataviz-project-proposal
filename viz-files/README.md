# Dataset: IMDB Top 250 Movies Dataset

**Source URL:**
[IMDB Top 250 Movies Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rajugc/imdb-top-250-movies-dataset)

**Original Source URL:**  
[IMDB List of Top 250 Movies](https://www.imdb.com/list/ls513400350/?sort=user_rating%2Cdesc)

## Description

IMDB (Internet Movie Database) is one of the largest online
databases for movies and television shows, providing
comprehensive information about movies, including ratings
and reviews from its vast user base. IMDB ratings are widely
used as a benchmark for the popularity and success of
movies.

This dataset contains the Top 250 rated movies on IMDB as of
2021, offering a snapshot of the most popular and highly
rated films. It includes details such as ranking, title,
year of release, rating, genres, runtime, certification,
budget, box office, casts, directors, writers, and taglines.

By analyzing this dataset, one can explore trends in cinema
such as rating distributions, financial performance,
dominant genres, and the influence of directors and actors
on a movie’s success.

## Attribute Types

- **rank**: Quantitative (ordered)
- **name**: Categorical
- **year**: Ordinal
- **rating**: Quantitative
- **genre**: Categorical
- **certificate**: Categorical
- **run_time**: Quantitative (time duration)
- **tagline**: Categorical
- **casts**: Categorical
- **directors**: Categorical
- **writers**: Categorical
